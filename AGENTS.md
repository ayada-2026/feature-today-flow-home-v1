## Working Style

- Keep the existing HTML/CSS/JavaScript structure.
- Focus on code edits and local/browser verification.

- Git commands may not be available in the Codex session, so do not rely on `git` or `gh`.

## Product Direction

- Preserve the warm beige, cream, peach, and soft stationery mood.
- Preserve the round day board, post-it overlays, today's rail, today's records, and stamp concept.
- Keep the app feeling light and diary-like, not like a rigid productivity tool.
- Avoid introducing a new framework or large architecture rewrite.

## Out Of Scope Unless Requested

- Supabase or external database integration.
- Login or account features.
- Complex date-based persistence.
- Major redesigns.
- New framework adoption.

## Verification
